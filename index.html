Latest News

New claim menu & Space Parkour

Posted by DizzyIts · October 30, 2026

Claiming land is a lot easier now. Type /claim in Survival to open the new claim menu:

  • Claim a square around you in one click and pick the size.
  • Trust friends by clicking their head.
  • See, show and delete all of your claims in one place.

New players now start with 1,000 claim blocks and get a claim stick when they first join.

We also opened the Space Parkour at spawn. Finish it for $1,000 and 200 bonus claim blocks (once a day). Type /parkour to give it a try.

Bedrock players can now join!

Posted by DizzyIts · October 29, 2026

You can now play DizzyMC from your phone, console or Windows 10/11. Bedrock and Java players share the same worlds, and Bedrock skins show up for Java players too.

See How to Join for the address and port.

Survival is open

Posted by DizzyIts · October 28, 2026

DizzyMC Survival is live! Here's some of what's waiting for you:

  • Spawners that store their loot, with a sell button
  • An AFK zone that pays $1,000 a minute
  • A PvP arena where the winner keeps the loot
  • Chest shops, the auction house, player warps and homes
  • Locks on chests, furnaces, shulker boxes and doors

Join with play.dizzymc.net and say hi in chat!
